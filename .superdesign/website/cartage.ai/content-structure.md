# Wilson

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** AI-powered logistics software for manufacturers and distributors
- **What they do:** Wilson automates freight operations—load creation, booking, tracking, shipment coordination—end-to-end via email, Slack, Teams, or ERP integration
- **Target audience:** Operations teams at mid-market manufacturers and distributors managing multiple shipments
- **Page goal:** Establish Wilson as an autonomous logistics agent; drive sign-ups via "Hire Me" CTA

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
**Visual character:** Playful personification. Centered white h3 "I'm Wilson, your last logistics hire" + body copy over a landscape road photo (1.30:1), with an illustrated green anthropomorphic package character (smiley face, hanging from a string) in center-bottom foreground. Slightly desaturated pastoral road/field background; character is matte green with strong depth. Two white buttons sit below the character: "Hire Wilson →" (solid white fill) and "Interview Wilson →" (white outline). Grid of 3 small visual icons (email, slack, spreadsheet) appears above buttons as sparse accent.

**Accent colors:** White text + buttons on near-black/dark olive backdrop; green character tie-in to brand. Hero fills full viewport height.

**Primary interaction:** Two dual-action CTAs—both route to /contact; "Hire" is primary (filled), "Interview" is secondary (outline).

### Header / Navigation
Horizontal, fixed-top. Cartage logo (text + underline accent) on left; center nav links = 4 items (Wilson, Case studies, Resources, About) + search icon; right side = Login link + "Hire Me →" CTA (white pill button with arrow). No hamburger. Links in gray; accent (blue) on hover implied. Search + Login share a hover state.

### Primary CTA / Input
"Hire Me →" button (white, rounded pill, arrow suffix) appears twice: in header (top-right, always visible) and in hero (center-bottom, beneath character). Both link to /contact. Main accent is white; background is dark olive/near-black in header, white-fill on dark bg in hero.

### Operator
- **Purpose:** Introduce Wilson's always-on monitoring and action layer—email, calls, Slack, Teams, spreadsheet integration; proactive alerts and task execution.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned h2 "Operator" + intro bold pair ("I'm always on." + description). Three h3 sub-benefits ("Wherever you work", "Always watching", "Always acting") stacked vertically with gray body copy beneath each. Right side: feature image (1.50:1 ratio) bleeding off-edge. "Learn more" link + arrow at bottom-left.

### Concierge
- **Purpose:** Remove comms friction—carrier calls, customer follow-ups, status updates handled by Wilson.
- **Layout:** Mirrors Operator layout (h2 + intro pair + 3 h3 benefits + body). Image side alternates right. "Learn more" link at bottom-right.

### Architect
- **Purpose:** Show learning/optimization tier—load consolidation, lane intelligence, continuous improvement.
- **Layout:** Mirrors Concierge (h2 + intro + 3 h3 benefits + body). Image side alternates left. "Learn more" link at bottom-left.

**Notable alternation:** Operator image-right → Concierge image-right → Architect image-left (L-R-L flip for visual variety; all three use 1.50:1 or 1.62:1 feature images).

### Wilson learns from the best
- **Purpose:** Establish service reliability model—human backup when things go wrong.
- **Layout:** Centered h2 + single gray body paragraph + "Learn more" link. Contextual: sits between feature tiers and case studies to build trust before social proof.

### Case studies
- **Purpose:** Social proof via 8 customer stories across furniture, specialized, commodities verticals.
- **Layout:** Section headline "Case studies" + h2 "Wilson has helped companies across the US streamline their freight operations" + "Explore case studies →" link. Logo wall (10+ customer logos, gray, light opacity) below. Horizontal scrollable slider (Content Slider region, 8 cards total) with alternating category tags (Furniture / Specialized / Commodities) + h3 case title + "Read more →" link per card. Each card uses 1.00:1 thumbnail + text overlay or stacked layout.

### Newsroom
- **Purpose:** Drive awareness of company updates, AI industry news, Cartage thought leadership.
- **Layout:** h2 "Newsroom" + tagline "Behind the moves: industry, AI, and Cartage updates". Link collection below (truncated in accessibility tree; assume 4–6 links to external/internal news items, brown text, gray hover state).

### Footer CTA
- **Purpose:** Final conversion push for undecided users; reinforce service-backed model.
- **Layout:** Full-width dark-background section (brown). Left-aligned white h1 "You've got the vision. Wilson handles the freight." + white body "Quoting, tracking, coordination—off your plate, without the handover headaches." + white "Book a demo →" button (rounded pill, white fill). Right side features an image of a smart thermostat (smiley face, time display 7:30, temp 68°F) on wood surface with blurred green plant foreground (1.25:1 ratio, high contrast). Below: white subheading "Your personal AI logistics coordinator, backed by humans."

### Footer
- **Purpose:** Navigation, legal, social links, copyright.
- **Layout:** Horizontal link grid: Features (Operator, Concierge, Architect) | Resources (Case studies, Newsroom) | Legal (Privacy Policy, Terms of Usage) | Socials (LinkedIn). White text on brown background. Bottom line: left = "Copyright © Cartage, 2025"; right = "Made by OO" (gray, smaller).

**Notable patterns:** 
- Three feature sections (Operator, Concierge, Architect) use alternating image placement (L-R-L) and consistent h2 + intro-pair + 3×h3 benefits + body structure.
- Case studies slider = horizontal scroll with 8 uniform cards, category tags, and "Read more" links; preceded by logo wall (10 logos, uniform spacing, gray).
- Hero uses centered character with dual-button CTAs (filled + outline); no asymmetry.
- Footer CTA section reverses layout (text left, image right) with high-contrast brown background to signal final offer.
- Color dominance: white 89.5%, brown 10.5% (footer + lower-page section).
- All CTAs (Hire Me, Learn more, Explore, Book a demo) use arrow suffix and rounded pill shape where applicable.
